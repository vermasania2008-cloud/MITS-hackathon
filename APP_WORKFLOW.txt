APP WORKFLOW
============

Run: streamlit run frontend.py

1. frontend.py starts the Streamlit dashboard.
2. backend.py creates/reads data/sustainability.db.
3. The backend loads the latest 30 days and previous 30 days.
4. It calculates totals, comparisons, and data-based insights.
5. The frontend displays KPIs, charts, records, and CSV downloads.

New databases contain labelled sample data by default. Office records are
saved with backend.save_record({...}). Carbon estimates require an optional,
authoritative electricity emissions factor.
